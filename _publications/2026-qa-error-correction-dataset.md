The work constructs 9,958 examples for academic-regulation question answering, pairing draft answers with review feedback and final answers under PASS, FAIL, and OOS labels. Preliminary experiments show that correction performance depends on the quality of the review signal: supplying ground-truth feedback enables effective correction, while errors in learned feedback limit the combined system.
