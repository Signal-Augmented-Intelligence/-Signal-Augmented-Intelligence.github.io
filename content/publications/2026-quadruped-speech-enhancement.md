A 170-sample ego-noise database is recorded on a Unitree Go2 robot across site and ground, motion and microphone-position conditions. A DeepFilterNet3-based model trained with this ego-noise improves STOI from 0.87 to 0.90 and PESQ from 2.31 to 2.77 over a model trained on environmental noise only, in both English and Korean.
